# Community conduct

We want a practical, welcoming project where contributors can discuss language, accessibility, media, and software quality without personal attacks or discrimination.

Be respectful, provide reproducible evidence, protect source-material privacy, and distinguish facts from preferences. Harassment, doxxing, threats, discriminatory language, and publishing another person's private media or personal information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users, or the project. Serious conduct concerns can be reported through the repository's private security-reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as private as reasonably possible.
